Welcome to DigItAnt – The Platform for the Languages of Ancient Italy

DigItAnt is an innovative platform designed to support scholars and researchers in the creation, management, and consultation of digital linguistic resources concerning the languages of Ancient Italy, languages documented almost exclusively in epigraphic texts. DigItAnt integrates and connects various sources of information, enabling their exploration in a unified and easily accessible environment.


DigItAnt has been developed within the project "Languages and Cultures of Ancient Italy. Historical Linguistics and Digital Models” (PRIN 2017 Program), funded by the Ministry of Universities and Research as part of the PRIN 2017 program.
